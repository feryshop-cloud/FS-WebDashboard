# Deals

**Mode:** Operate

Buy/sell deal management with split payments. Supports lunas (full payment), booking/DP, cicilan/pelunasan (installments), trade-in, cancel, and refund flows. Automatic tracking of total-paid, remaining, percentage, and jatuh tempo (due date).

## Sections

- Deal list with status filters
- Deal creation with split payment support
- Deal detail view with payment timeline
- Status management (lunas, booking, cancel, refund)
