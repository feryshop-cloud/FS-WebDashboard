# Trade-in / Tukar Tambah

**Mode:** Operate

Trade-in management for customer account exchanges. Supports multiple incoming customer accounts, cash top-up or cashback. Trade-in value is kept strictly separate from rekening balances.

## Sections

- Trade-in list
- Incoming account tracking
- Cash top-up / cashback management
- Trade-in value separation from rekening
